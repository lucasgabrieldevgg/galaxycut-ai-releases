# v1.6.0 — The Agent Builds The Timeline

**Released:** 2026-10-30

- THE AGENT BUILDS THE TIMELINE: a new tool — timeline_add_clip — lets the AI assemble your media onto the timeline by itself: it resolves the file by name (with honest refusals listing candidates when names are ambiguous), routes it by TYPE (video to V1, image to the overlay track, audio to A1 — an mp3 never lands on a video track again), and positions it without ever stacking on top of another clip (finds the free slot or appends at the end, and tells you which). The registry grows from 58 to 59 official tools, and the bridge exposes it automatically. You asked the app to stop making you drag everything by hand — this is that answer.
- IMPORT WITHOUT THE SURPRISE DIALOG: media_import_local_folder now honors its folder_path parameter — files inside your granted agent folder are imported BY PATH with a real media probe (same physics as the folder tools), and the media KIND is derived from the actual extension/MIME: an mp3 is audio, a png is an image, no more 'videos' that are actually your narration. The native file dialog only opens when you explicitly ask for it (use_dialog: true) — it will never steal your focus uninvited again.
- THE BRIDGE SEES THE WORK: ponte_status now serves live jobs[] with progress quantized in steps of 10 (0, 10, 20… 100 only when done) — transcription and export are visible to the external AI while they run, ending the 105-205s blind window where the agent repeated numbers it could not see. And when a worker finishes AFTER the turn deadline, the result is preserved as a 'late' job and delivered on the next poll — computed work never dies in limbo again.
- ANSWERS THAT ARRIVE WHOLE: your replies to the agent's questions are now served COMPLETE to the external AI through ponte_get_context (with acknowledgment), instead of routing to an internal Director with no model and arriving truncated. Ghost plans die on fresh start (a plan from a dead session revalidates its targets once, with the real names on the timeline, instead of demanding deviations forever). And get_context no longer recommends tools the bridge itself forbids — the map the external AI reads never contradicts the roads it can actually take.
- THE EAR DOES NOT INVENT SPEECH: every transcription now passes through whisperGuard — timestamps outside the clip's real duration are clamped or rejected (the impossible 61.82s in a 60.1s clip can't happen again), 'no speech' is decided by MEASURED silence (RMS), not guessed, and each result carries an honest verdict: ok, suspect, reject, or no-speech. The source is DECLARED (the clip you asked for, or the timeline mix in the window) and the session cache is invalidated automatically when the edit changes shape — the transcriber can no longer silently serve you the wrong audio.
- THE CUT THAT CHECKS ITSELF: after remove_silences or the now-REAL remove_fillers (it actually cuts the filler ranges and rebuilds the clip), the edited audio is re-transcribed and compared with the session's previous reading — the result says confirmed or contradiction, distinct from not-checked (pattern adopted with credit from the claude-video-editor reference). And for those with RAM to spare: an optional whisper-local-server (same mold as the demucs one) serves large-v3 quality word-level transcription — without it, the offline base model keeps working with its honest notes.
