# v1.5.4 — The Pairing Race Is Dead

**Released:** 2026-10-24

- THE PAIRING RACE IS DEAD: in v1.5.2/v1.5.3 the first poll of the Modo Ponte left the app ~50 ms after “Conectar e gerar o bloco” — while POST /pair was still creating the channel at the relay (measured 2.3 s against production). The poll took a 401 (channel not born yet) and the client declared the pairing REVOKED with a terminal halt — the channel was born orphaned and nothing ever polled it again. That is the whole field saga in one number pair: 8 pairings in the database, ZERO polls, ZERO results. The connect button now runs the full ceremony BEFORE touching the store: generate tokens → register at the relay → only on 200 activate the pairing. Order became structure, not timing
- A 401 BEFORE FIRST CONTACT IS NO LONGER A DEATH SENTENCE: the transport used to treat any 401 as terminal revocation. Now, while the channel has never answered (pre-contact), a 401 opens a 60 s grace window with honest backoff — the panel says disconnected-and-retrying, not revoked. Real contact resets the window; a 401 AFTER contact is still terminal on the spot (P11 untouched). Window exhausted without contact = the old verdict, just late
- THE HUB CARD NOW REGISTERS AT THE RELAY: the “Conectar” of the Ponte provider card in Modelos & APIs minted a pairing state without EVER calling /pair — tokens the relay had never seen (the second family of field 401s). The card now runs the same generate → register → activate ceremony with its own “Registrando no relay…” state and honest errors on failure. Both pairing surfaces call the same relayPairing module — they can't diverge anymore
- A FAILED REGISTRATION NO LONGER KILLS THE OLD PAIRING: if /pair fails (timeout, relay down), the previously active pairing stays untouched — the unpair left the error path on purpose. You just try again; your working channel survives the retry
- THE FIELD PROOF LIVES IN THE SOURCE: the header of the new relayPairing module records the production measurement that nailed the bug (pair completed in 2.28 s while the first poll, fired 50 ms after the click, got its 401 in 1.96 s) — the race was proven against the live relay, not guessed. The full diagnosis (lastSeenAgent frozen at createdAt on every channel ever paired) is in the session worklog
