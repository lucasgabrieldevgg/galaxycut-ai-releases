# v1.5.6 — The UI Went To Finishing School

**Released:** 2026-10-25

- THE UI WENT TO FINISHING SCHOOL (WAVE 2): every native title= tooltip in the house died and was reborn as a real Radix tooltip (instant, keyboard-aware, one consistent engine) — frame, rail, panels, PlanCard, Timeline, ClipProperties and the composer. The TopNav menu is now a Radix dropdown: the last hand-written click-outside machine is gone, and focus trap, Esc, arrows and highlight came for free. The ui/ primitive shelf grew from 4 to 16 components, and the AgentCommandCenter — the bot's entire brain — was cut in half (1725 → 938 lines, −46%) by moving its 12 sub-cards into a proper acc-cards/ family plus TurnList, usePonteExternalTurn and useAutoExport hooks, every move proven byte-identical by script. The orphan TakeoverControls file left with it (Stop stays in the composer, Revert stays per turn — the brake never lived there)
- MOTION CINEMA, NOW WITH REAL PHYSICS: the code layer gained a dampedSpring closed-form oscillator — a pure function whose stiffness and damping you can turn by hand — plus 6 new film-school templates on top of the 10 from v1.5.5: impact_frame (rhythmic hits with shake+flash+zoom, reborn speed-lines and manga hold-frames, impactCount 1..5), chromatic_glitch (RADIAL RGB aberration with 4-frame surges, per-slice blocks and CRT scanlines), comic_wipe (ink panels with a diagonal wipe, deterministic halftone and sparkles), kinetic_mask (per-word mask reveal with alternating skew and a curtain that walks away), counter_rich (count-up with a CHOOSABLE easing — spring delegates to the real physics — prefix/suffix/pt-BR thousands separator, ring+bar) and logo_stinger (monogram cut by a vertical mask, sweeping light, tracking 2.0→1, signature particles, collapse to a line). 16 templates in total
- SEE THE MOTION BEFORE YOU COMMIT: the ClipPropertiesPanel gained a 'Motion cinema · física & efeito' section with 7 live controls (intensity, easing, direction, impact count, prefix/suffix/decimals…) writing the REAL clip.code — and a catalog of animated mini-canvases (MotionCatalogPreview) running the very same drawCodeLayer at ~30fps: preview and export draw the exact same frame, so what loops in the catalog is what lands in the MP4. The agent side got honest too: typewriter left the kinetic enum (it promised what it never drew — errata committed), and golden rule #9 now makes the agent ASK which motion style you want before applying (same ruler as subtitles)
- THE AUDIO IS REAL NOW: the six audio tools that honestly refused to fake it now execute. audio_insert_sfx synthesizes 8 SFX types deterministically (same type, same bytes — real audio clips in the Effects track). audio_add_background_music composes a procedural 5-mood bed (its own BPM, harmony and arrangement) spanning the whole timeline, measured and normalized to your target loudness (ITU-R BS.1770-4). audio_apply_auto_ducking is ONE gain polygon: the render schedules it, the preview evaluates it — the music bends under speech, never disappears. audio_separate_stems does honest mid/side DSP with a vocal band (karaoke-grade, declared on the tin: not AI). audio_normalize_loudness measures LUFS on the decoded PCM and writes the gain on the clip's REAL volume. subtitle_highlight_keywords burns impact keywords into the subtitle props — color/box/glow from birth, pop on the word's exact time, drawn by the one and only drawTextLayer
- THE BRIDGE LEARNED THE NEW CRAFT: the ponte skill (app + production relay, already deployed and serving) catalogs the 16 motion templates with their parameter grammar (stiffness 1..12, damping 0.05..1, intensity, direction, easing) and golden rule #9 — ask the motion style first. The registry's audio entries are now honest about what they DO (real execution, real bytes in the timeline). Behind the curtains: audioTools.ts is a 1092-line pure Node-safe library with zero Math.random (byte-a-byte determinism), the verify suite grew to 27 scripts (293 new assertions across onda2//), and (NVIDIA NIM catalog) was killed while (model downloads) moved to the ModelCenter backlog — no dead weight in this release
