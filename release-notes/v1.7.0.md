# v1.7.0 — The Preview Sings

**Released:** 2026-10-31

- THE PREVIEW SINGS: press play and HEAR the timeline — the voice, the music and the SFX the AI assembled all play in the preview now, no export required to know what you have. Every clip gets its own live audio element (voice on A1, music on A2, SFX in place), so a sound-only timeline — narration, music and captions with no video track — is no longer a silent structure: it sings while you watch. And what you hear is what you get: the preview and the export drink from the same audio engine, same gain curves, same truth. You said you could not hear ANY sound — this is that answer.
- ONE CLOCK, ONE TRUTH: the preview now runs on a single master clock. Before, a clip with a speed change played at 1x in the preview but sped up in the export (two truths), and when a video clip ended the playhead could freeze mid-play with the button stuck on 'playing'. Both ghosts are dead: speed follows the same math the export uses, the playhead walks on real time, and pause means pause everywhere.
- THE VOICE ENHANCER, IN THE AGENT'S HAND: a new tool — audio_enhance_voice, the 63rd official — puts the editor's 'Padrão OBS Voz Clara' chain in the AI's hand. One call enhances a clip or EVERY voice carrier at once (music beds and SFX spared), with zero studio parameters to guess: the chain is locked exactly as the editor built it — high-pass at 85 Hz, a gentle compressor, a safety limiter, a touch of makeup gain. The panel toggle and the AI write the SAME field (never two truths), and the offline export now honors it too — before, the export ignored the enhance flag even when you set it by hand in the editor.
- MEDIA LANDS ON THE RIGHT TRACK: the agent and you follow the SAME routing rule — audio to A1, image to the overlay track, video to V1. An mp3 attached after a video never lands on the video track again, and nothing stacks on top of another clip (the agent finds the free slot or appends at the end, and says which). timeline_update_clip can move a clip between tracks with the same guard the human drag has, narration clips are defended from edits that would mute them, and media lookup by name is unified across every tool.
- THE FOLDER ICON IS ALWAYS THERE: the folder icon lives in the composer corner, always visible on desktop — granting the agent folder is one click, and /pasta stops being the only door (it was a command you had to know to open what you asked for). And the out-of-folder search became a real permission level: scoped to at most 5 folders, every use audited in the worklog.
