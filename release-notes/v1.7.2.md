# v1.7.2 — The App Is For Everyone

**Released:** 2026-11-01

- THE APP IS FOR EVERYONE: your personal editing skill no longer ships inside the app. The bridge contract now teaches only the generic craft — the tools, the tracks, the entrance animations (fadeIn, spinIn, zoomIn, popIn, typewriter, 0.1-3s), the subtitle style overrides (any TextProps key, position as a fraction from the top), the voice enhancer — and one clean rule: if you bring your own style skill (pasted in the chat or imported in the app), IT decides the style; the contract decides the tools. The built-in script models got the same surgery: instead of directing videos in one specific personal format, they follow whatever format skill the user imported, sovereign. Your format is yours again — integrate it wherever you want; the app serves everyone the same honest toolkit.
- NO MORE GRAY SCREEN AFTER UPDATING: you said the app restarts after an update and stays gray — it has to open as if freshly opened in the new version. Root cause found and killed: the app serves its pages from the same local origin every launch (that is why your timeline survives restarts), which also means the old version's rendering caches (GPU, code cache) survive the update and can poison the new renderer — and until now a dead renderer had no self-healing at all: gray window, forever. Now, when the version changes, those render caches are cleared before the first window opens (your projects, timeline and settings are never touched), and the renderer gained a full immune system: a crash reloads the window (up to 3 times), a stubborn crash triggers one clean rescue relaunch per 10 minutes (anti-loop guard), a failed page load retries, and if nothing loads within 20 seconds the watchdog reloads. Opening after an update now IS opening fresh in the new version.
- MEDIA THAT SURVIVES THE NIGHT (spec dispatch): you closed the app overnight and the next day the timeline was intact but every clip had lost its media, and the agent found nothing. The wound is measured and the fix is specified: imported media will be copied into the project's own durable folder, the media library index will be auto-saved silently alongside the timeline (no saving... saved indicator, exactly as you asked — only failures surface), and reopening will re-attach everything from the durable copies, which also restores the agent's eyes after a restart. The task is dispatched for implementation.
