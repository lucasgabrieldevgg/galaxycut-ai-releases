# v1.5.9 — The Timeline Survives The Restart

**Released:** 2026-10-30

- THE TIMELINE SURVIVES THE RESTART: close the app, open it again, and your timeline is ALIVE — every clip back on its track at its time, with the media re-found through four fallback channels (embedded blob → last local path → file name in your agent folder → clip name), so moving a file on disk no longer orphans your edit. When the media really is gone the clip comes back as an HONEST orphan (visible, named, replaceable) instead of silently dying. The 'what you see is what you reopen' rule of the pitch deck is now literally true: projects are files, and files keep their timelines.
- THE AGENT'S EYES: a new read-only tool — timeline_capture_frame — lets the agent SEE a frame of the timeline it just built. The loop that was audit-by-numbers becomes audit-see-fix: visual audit reports a suspect (cut text, overlap, empty scene), the agent captures that exact frame, looks at it, and fixes the cause — then re-audits. Honest limits, stated up front: 8 frame decodes per 180s (cache hits don't count), JPEG at most 640px wide, decoding happens in a hidden Chromium window and never touches your files. External AIs get the frame too: the pairing block served by the relay now teaches the capture contract, so the same eyes work through the bridge.
- SEVEN CINEMA OVERLAYS: the motion catalog grows from 16 to 23 templates — cinema_lower (the documentary lower-third), comment_card, follow_cta, music_card, post_card (social proof as motion), anamorphic_flare and liquid_glass (the two hero looks). Same grammar as every template before them (motion_* params on the update), same deterministic engine, zero randomness — what you preview is what you export.
- EIGHT CAMERA MOVES, FINALLY HONEST: whipPan (1.5W dash with a 10-sample smear), dollyZoom (the subject stays INVARIANT while the background solves the focal length — real Vertigo math), beatFreezeCut (ramp → freeze → flash → hard cut that lands on 1.0 EXACT), dissolveFilm (ordered Bayer dither, never random noise), pushUp (whole card out with proven overshoot), radial, scalePunch (2.5x blow-up) and blurCross (counter-drift crossfade). One envelope feeds BOTH media and text transitions — the same physics for everything on the timeline.
- BROADCAST LOUDNESS IN THE EXPORT: the finished mix now goes through an honest BS.1770-4 stage — integrated loudness of the WHOLE mix measured, linear gain to target, sample-peak clamp with 0.5 dB headroom (declared as an approximation of true-peak, no precision theater), and a note in plain words: 'X LUFS measured → target Y (gain Z dB)'. Silence gets no phantom gain. AND PLATFORM PRESETS: pick TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube or Universal in the export hub and the resolution, bitrate, target loudness and the DEAD ZONE (that footer nobody can see, in real pixels of YOUR canvas) all calibrate at once — the visual audit now warns when a caption sits inside the zone where thumbs cover the screen. Your choice persists and flows to the agent's own exports.
- AN EDITOR'S REFLEXES: silence removal is now ADAPTIVE per block (margins clamp between 0.5x and 1.5x — the house default stays the historical fixed cut) · music separation via local Demucs when YOU run the bundled server (an honest 'not implemented' when it's off — the model never pretends to live inside the app) · motion analysis refuses BEFORE decoding instead of burning your eyes budget · and the agent reads two new skills: cinema-arsenal (all 23 motion templates by name, with the combination grammar) and shorts-regua-de-mercado (the market ruler — 15s retains 92%, 30s 84%, 60s 68%; a 1s hook at 70%+ of the frame doubles completion; pacing 1-3s; 70-80 words per 30s; dead zones per platform) applied to every scripted workflow. Your Dynamic Shorts spec stays sovereign — the ruler talks TO it, never edits it.
