## v1.7.13 — The Honest Measurement Release

**Released:** 2026-11-21

- THE HONEST MEASUREMENT RELEASE — every item here came from the field asking three questions the app could not answer: what does the frame LOOK like, what does the audio DO, and what did the command ACTUALLY do. The answers were lies (or silence): the mirror returned 'toDataURL did not return an image', the audio was never measured, the pacing promised cuts and only retimed, the normalize said '0% in 5 min' while measuring the whole timeline, the export said 'done' without saying what it recorded. v1.7.13 replaces the guesses with measurements.
- THE MIRROR ACTUALLY MIRRORS: timeline_capture_composite was born with a 0×0 canvas. The tool passed width: Number(params.width); with the parameter omitted that is NaN, NaN is not nullish so the ?? 480 default never held, the clamp handed NaN back, and the canvas width IDL (unsigned long) turned it into 0 — toDataURL on a canvas with no area has no image to give. Now the width only travels when it is FINITE (default 480 from the schema), the canvas can never leave without area, and EVERY failure names its own cause: no area (InvalidStateError) is not media-from-another-origin (SecurityError/CORS) is not an empty read. A wrong cause sends the owner reimporting media that was perfect.
- THE SCHEMA STOPS LYING ABOUT THE PITCH: timeline_apply_speed_ramping accepted preserve_pitch (default true) while NO implementation existed anywhere in the engine — the external AI asked for the pitch to be kept, believed it was kept, and the export changed it anyway (the audio plays through an AudioBufferSourceNode, and playbackRate without a time-stretch changes the frequency). The parameter is GONE from the schema and the description now says the truth: the speed ramp CHANGES THE TONE. A schema that promises what the engine cannot do is the same bug class the pacing was and the normalize was — and a real pitch-preserving time-stretch is a feature, not a schema fix.
- THE AUDIO IS MEASURED: the AI could not hear the result, so it guessed. Now the engine measures the envelope, the speech, the silences and the syllable attacks, and hands the numbers over. The field measured 45-236 ms of real execution and 1.5 s of poll per command — the app was never the bottleneck, the measurement was.
- THE WHISPER BUDGETS ITSELF: the word-sync promised ~2 minutes and the field lost 164 s to a 150 s turn ceiling with nothing to show. Now the budget is declared BEFORE the spend, the timeout is rethrown instead of swallowed into another full-length retry, and a late job keeps its full payload instead of a comma summary.
- THE CUT RETURNS THE MAP: the cut returns windowSpeech — the speech of the WINDOW that was cut, not of the whole file. A map you can navigate beats a number you cannot.
- THE EXPORT SAYS WHAT IT RECORDED: an undecodable mp4 dies in the message that says what was actually written to disk — file, size, duration — instead of a success that lies.
- GET_CLIPS SAYS WHAT IT DID NOT RETURN: the empty list now names the clips it could not bridge and why, instead of looking like an empty timeline.
- THE PLAN DOES NOT POISON: an orphan step dies alone; deviation is the normal path, not a blocked session. plan_skip_step unblocks the build without faking the work.
- THE NORMALIZE SAYS SCOPE AND PRICE: '0% in 5 min' is dead. The normalize declares the scope it measured (clip_id/track_index) and the price it paid (PCM x clock) before it touches the audio.
- THE PACING IS RETIME: the pacing promised an average cut cadence and delivered a fixed 1.5x speed ramp — the video came out accelerated while the AI believed it had cut. Now the pacing accelerates the takes TOWARD the target, under the preset ceiling (mrbeast_fast 1.6s/2.5x, educational_balanced 3.0s/1.75x, cinematic_calm 6.0s/1.25x, custom requires a target), it reports the average duration before/after, the RESIDUAL (how far the clip stayed from the target) and cuts_created: 0 — because a cut that does not change the video is not a cut, it is a counted lie. The source offset travels with it (the source offset module): the second half of a split used to replay from the beginning of the file.
- PROOF IS PART OF THE RELEASE: the verify suite grew 64 -> 66 (verify--espelho-composite, verify--preserve-pitch) and every count that moved was re-anchored WITH ITS CAUSE written inside the proof — a number without a cause is a number that lies. typecheck clean; the lint baseline of the untouched files stays untouched.

## v1.7.10 — The Mount-Failure Cure

**Released:** 2026-11-03

- THE MOUNT-FAILURE CURE (owner's screenshot, 03/11 00:31: 'Cannot mount AppImage, please check your FUSE setup.'). The v1.7.9 runtime only caught MISSING libfuse2 / missing /dev/fuse — but the owner's machine has both, and the mount itself fails (broken fusermount, permissions, container, kernel without fuse). Every AppImage we ever shipped (v1.4.1-v1.7.9) dies on that path: the runtime notifies the scary dialog and the app never opens. This release closes the LAST pre-Electron death path.
- FORENSIC FINDING (why detection was hard): the squashfuse keepalive byte is emitted even when fuse_mount FAILS — sqfs_ll_mount does not propagate the NULL, the callback runs anyway, and the parent believes the mount succeeded. The only trustworthy signal is whether AppRun is actually VISIBLE at the mountpoint. The v2 runtime checks exactly that.
- RUNTIME gc-no-fuse-2: if the mount did not produce a live AppRun at the mountpoint, the parent cleans up the empty mount dir and re-executes itself with --appimage-extract-and-run (the official extraction path, content-aware cache, no FUSE at all). The child's dialog now says 'GalaxyCut AI — FUSE is broken on this system - opening WITHOUT FUSE' instead of 'Cannot mount AppImage'. With a healthy FUSE setup, nothing changes: standard mounting, byte-for-byte the upstream flow.
- REPRODUCED AND PROVEN BEFORE SHIPPING (the owner's rule): the owner's exact scenario was rebuilt in a hostile sandbox — REAL libfuse2 2.9.9 loaded (dlopen OK), /dev/fuse present (access OK), mount impossible. v1.7.9 there: mount error, execv error, exit 127, zero beacons (the silent double-click killer). The v1.7.10 runtime in the same environment: detects the dead mount, falls back to extraction, AppRun executes, exit 0.
- PIPELINE NOTE: appimagetool continuous (Nov/2026 build) dropped gzip and xz from its bundled mksquashfs (zstd only — our runtime reads gzip/xz). The repack step now uses a gzip-capable mksquashfs (squashfs-tools 4.6.1) via PATH, keeping the AppImage 100% standard gzip/type-2.
